Pixel's company system lets you team up with other server members to build a shared economy entity. Companies can pool funds, invite new members, and compete for rankings.

## Creating a Company

Run `,company create <name>` to form a new company. You will become the founder and can invite others to join.

Example:

```javascript
,company create Pixel Ventures
```

## Managing Members and Funds

| Command | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `,company invite @user` | Invite a member to join your company |
| `,company deposit <amount>` | Add personal coins to the company balance |
| `,company leaderboard` | View the top companies by total value |

<Warning>
  Deposited coins become company property. In most configurations, only the founder or designated officers can withdraw or manage funds. Check your server rules before depositing large amounts.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Invite active members who play mini-games and collect dailies. A larger, active company grows faster and ranks higher on the leaderboard.
</Tip>
