Pixel's business feature lets you buy a virtual business that generates passive income over time. Once purchased, you can collect earnings and upgrade the business to increase its output.

## Buying a Business

Run `,business buy <name>` to purchase your first business. Choose a name that fits your theme or brand within the server.

Example:

```text
,business buy Pixel Pizza
```

## Managing Your Business

| Command | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `,business collect` | Collect accumulated earnings |
| `,business upgrade` | Spend coins to increase income rate |
| `,business info` | View current level, earnings, and upgrade cost |

Earnings accumulate even while you are offline, so remember to collect regularly. Upgrades raise the amount generated per cycle but cost more at higher levels.

<Note>
  You can only own one business at a time. Choose your name carefully, or ask a server admin if renames are supported.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Set a reminder to collect earnings daily. Passive income adds up quickly when combined with active gameplay like mini-games and company activities.
</Tip>
