Pixel's laboratory lets you research and craft items that boost your economy progress. Crafting requires materials and time, but the results can improve your income, luck, or other stats.

## Research and Crafting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start research">
    Run `,lab research <item>` to begin researching a new recipe. Research takes time to complete and unlocks the ability to craft that item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Craft the item">
    Once research is finished, use `,lab craft <item>` to build it. Crafting consumes materials from your inventory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check inventory">
    Run `,lab inventory` to see your researched recipes, materials, and crafted items.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding Timers and Materials

Each item has a research duration and a material cost. You can only research one item at a time in most configurations. Materials are earned through economy activities like games, daily rewards, or business income.

<Note>
  Research timers run in real time. You do not need to stay online, but you must wait for research to finish before crafting the item.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Prioritize researching income boosters first. They pay for themselves over time by increasing your overall earning rate.
</Tip>
