Pixel's card system adds a collectible trading card game to your server economy. Cards appear as drops that you can claim, store, and trade with other members.

## Finding and Claiming Cards

Cards spawn randomly in the server as drops. When a drop appears, use `,cards claim <id>` to add it to your inventory before someone else claims it.

Run `,cards drop` to check for active drops or trigger a new drop cycle, depending on your server configuration.

## Managing Your Collection

| Command | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `,cards inventory` | View your collected cards |
| `,cards trade @user` | Initiate a trade with another member |

Cards come in rarity tiers. Higher rarity cards are harder to find and more valuable in trades.

<Note>
  Drop timing and rarity rates are set by your server admins. Some servers limit how often drops appear to keep the economy balanced.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Watch chat closely after `,cards drop`. Drops are first-come, first-served, and popular servers move fast.
</Tip>
