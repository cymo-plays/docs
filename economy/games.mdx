Pixel's mini-games let you wager your economy currency for a chance to win more. Each game has its own odds and style, so you can pick the one that fits your risk preference.

## Available Games

| Command | Game | How It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `,coinflip` | Coinflip | Wager on heads or tails for a 50/50 chance to double your bet |
| `,slots <amount>` | Slots | Spin the reels. Match symbols to win multipliers |
| `,blackjack <amount>` | Blackjack | Play against the dealer to get closest to 21 without busting |
| `,roulette <color or number> <amount>` | Roulette | Bet on a color (red/black) or a specific number for higher payouts |

## Example Commands

```js
,coinflip 100
,slots 50
,blackjack 200
,roulette red 100
,roulette 17 100
```

<Warning>
  These games involve wagering your economy balance. Only bet what you are willing to lose. Set personal limits and avoid chasing losses.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Start with small bets to learn each game's flow. Coinflip offers the simplest odds, while roulette number bets offer the highest payouts but the lowest win chance.
</Tip>
