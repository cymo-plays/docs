Pixel's economy system adds a virtual currency layer to your Discord server. Members can earn coins, trade with each other, and compete for the top spot on the economy leaderboard.

## Core Economy Commands

| Command | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `,balance` | Check your current coin balance |
| `,daily` | Claim your daily reward (resets every 24 hours) |
| `,pay @user <amount>` | Send coins to another member |
| `,leaderboard economy` | View the richest members in the server |

## Earning Currency

The fastest way to build your balance is to claim `,daily` consistently. You can also earn coins through mini-games, businesses, and company activities covered in the other economy guides.

<Note>
  Daily rewards stack with streaks in some configurations. Check your server settings or ask an admin if streak bonuses are enabled.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use `,pay` to reward members for participation, contests, or helpful behavior. Transactions are logged and visible to server staff.
</Tip>
