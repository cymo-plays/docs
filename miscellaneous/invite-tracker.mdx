Pixel's invite tracker records which members invited new users to your server. This is useful for reward systems, verification, or simply understanding where your growth comes from.

## Viewing Invite Stats

Use `,invites @user` to see a breakdown of any member's invites. The stats include four categories:

- **Regular:** Users who joined and are still in the server.
- **Left:** Users who joined but have since left.
- **Fake:** Users who were flagged as alt accounts or duplicates.
- **Bonus:** Extra invites manually added by staff.

## Leaderboards and Management

| Command | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `,invites @user` | View invite stats for a specific member |
| `,invites leaderboard` | Show the top inviters in the server |
| `,invites reset @user` | Reset a member's invite count to zero |
| `,invites bonus @user <amount>` | Add bonus invites to a member |

<Warning>
  Resetting invites cannot be undone. Make sure you want to clear the data before running `,invites reset`.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Use bonus invites to reward members for events or contests without requiring them to generate actual invite links.
</Tip>
